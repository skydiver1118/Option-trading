FRAMEWORK

Two-layer decision system

1

Ownership gate

Only Stock V2 Long-Term BUY or STRONG BUY underlyings can ever receive a short-put SELL.

2

Canonical technical state

Covered stocks use the same Stock V2 daily RSI, trend and support layer rather than independently reclassifying the underlying.

3

Option economics

IV, premium yield, delta, DTE, breakeven-to-support and bid/ask liquidity are scored independently.

4

Execution control

Support proximity, stabilization and earnings/event risk can keep an otherwise attractive stock at WAIT.
